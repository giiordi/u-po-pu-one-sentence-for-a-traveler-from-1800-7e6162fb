# One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb)
[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/entrant/giiordi)](https://make.fun/@giiordi)
[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/referral/v0wjehd6)](https://make.fun/?ref=v0wjehd6)

One sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to
a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from
things they already know: libraries, post offices, roads, letters, books.

> Imagine every library and post office on earth joined by one road, so a
> letter crosses the sea in a blink and any book you name opens in your
> hands.

`main.ts` (Node, stdlib only) validates the gate — one line, 1–200
characters, no leading or trailing whitespace, no word coined after 1800 —
and writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`), with no trailing newline.
